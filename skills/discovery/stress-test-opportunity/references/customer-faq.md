# Customer FAQ Stage

Use this stage to test whether a skeptical customer would believe the value,
trust the experience, and adopt it over their current workaround.

## Required Question Areas

Adapt wording to the concept, but cover these areas:

- Why should this customer care now?
- What does this replace, reduce, or improve?
- Why is it better than the current workaround?
- What proof, evidence, or source material backs the output?
- What happens when it is wrong, incomplete, delayed, or unavailable?
- What does the customer still need to do manually?
- What are the privacy, security, compliance, or trust implications?
- Who should not use it yet?
- What is excluded from V1?
- How will a customer know it is working?

## Conditional Trust Model

For AI, automation, generated analysis, sensitive data, or source-system
synthesis, require answers to:

- Why should users trust the output?
- What evidence is shown beside claims?
- What happens when confidence is low?
- What remains human-reviewed or human-controlled?
- What is the cost of a wrong answer?
- How can users correct, override, or ignore the result?

## Coaching Rules

- Ask one FAQ decision at a time when the answer affects scope or trust.
- Do not let the FAQ become promotional copy.
- Label answers that depend on evidence as supported, user-stated, assumed,
  unproven, or false.
- Prefer honest constraints over vague confidence.
- When the user gives a broad answer, propose a narrower answer and ask them to
  accept or correct it.

## Exit Criteria

Move forward only when customer objections have produced:

- clearer value proposition
- adoption constraints
- trust/failure requirements
- observable success signal
- explicit V1 limits
